Stride scheduling

Proportional share without any randomness. Each job gets a stride, inversely proportional to its tickets, and a running total called its pass value. Whoever has the lowest pass runs next and then advances by its stride, following the Scheduling: Proportional Share chapter of OSTEP (Chapter 9).

The whole scheduler

Four lines, run once per time slice. No random numbers, and no recalculating anything globally.
