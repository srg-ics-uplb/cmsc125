Translating addresses with segmentation

A process with code, heap and stack segments, following the Segmentation chapter of OSTEP. Its 16KB address space uses 14-bit virtual addresses: bits 13 and 12 name the segment and the other 12 bits are the offset. The segments are placed in 64KB of physical memory.
